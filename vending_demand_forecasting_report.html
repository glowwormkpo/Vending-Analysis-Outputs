Method

The workflow follows standard time-series practice: convert date fields, aggregate by time period, inspect trend and seasonality, create calendar and lag-style features, evaluate a holdout forecast, and translate expected demand into reorder levels. The referenced articles emphasize stationarity, autocorrelation, decomposition, train/test forecasting, and error metrics such as RMSE.
